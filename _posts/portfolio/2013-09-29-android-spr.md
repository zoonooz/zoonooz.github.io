I made this app for Superrich as a freelance project. They needed to redesign their old app, which was made in 2011.

![image](/img/portfolio/spr1.jpg)
{: style="text-align: center"}

The app shows currency exchange rates for their customers. It can also calculate rates for selected currencies, show the latest news, and display a map of branches with contact information.

![image](/img/portfolio/spr2.jpg)
{: style="text-align: center"}

This app was downloaded more than 100k times and served users for 5 years.
