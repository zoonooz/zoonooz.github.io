When I was developing [Himabu](/portfolio/android-himabu), I wanted to give users full access to the service on as many platforms as possible. As Himabu was already available on iOS and Android, I decided to build this in my free time, since the company did not have enough resources to do it. Even though the company never released the app to the public, it worked well internally and greatly reduced the customer support team's effort when they needed to chat with users.

![image](/img/portfolio/himabu-desktop.png)
{: style="text-align: center"}

The app was built using **Electron** framework with **ReactJS** and **RxJS**. Thanks to Electron, the app runs on Windows and Linux as well.
