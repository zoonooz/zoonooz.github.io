I helped [Callstats](https://callstats.io) build libraries for Android and iOS that integrate with their RESTful API. Callstats collects [WebRTC](https://webrtc.org/) data, analyzes it, and shows it on a dashboard so that customers can detect anomalies and optimize their systems.

The library depends on Google's WebRTC library; it simply intercepts the events, organizes them, and sends them to Callstats.

**iOS** : [https://github.com/callstats-io/callstats-ios](https://github.com/callstats-io/callstats-ios)<br/>
**Android** : [https://github.com/callstats-io/callstats-android](https://github.com/callstats-io/callstats-android)
